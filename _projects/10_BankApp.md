<!-- Problem & Challenge -->
<div>
    <h2 class="mt-5 font-weight-bold">Problem & Challenge</h2>
    <p class="mt-3">The client's traditional online banking platform lacked a dedicated mobile interface for complex financial products like overseas bonds and funds. This created a significant user experience gap, forcing customers to use a desktop browser for transactions. Our challenge was to design and develop a secure, intuitive, and highly functional mobile interface that would empower users to easily manage their investments on the go.</p>
    <p>Our team faced three key challenges:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><b class="font-weight-bold">Information Hierarchy</b></li>
            How to present vast amounts of financial data (real-time bond prices, fund details) in a clear and digestible format on a small mobile screen.
        <li><b class="font-weight-bold">Transaction Security</b></li>
            Ensuring that sensitive financial transactions, such as transferring funds, were not only functional but also instilled a sense of security and trust in the user.
        <li><b class="font-weight-bold">Technical Integration</b></li>
            Translating complex back-end APIs into a seamless front-end experience.
    </ul>
</div>

<!-- My Process & Contributions -->
<div>
    <h2 class="mt-5 font-weight-bold">My Process & Contributions</h2>
    <p class="mt-3"> 
        I was responsible for the front-end development cycle of the overseas bond and fund trading feature. My work began by collaborating with the client to understand their business requirements and technical specifications.
    </p>
    <p>
        My key contributions were:
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li><b class="font-weight-bold">Front-End Development</b></li>
            I developed and maintained the mobile app's core user interface, focusing on creating a clean, logical flow for users to browse, search, and execute trades.
        <li><b class="font-weight-bold">API Integration</b></li>
            I successfully integrated the front-end with the back-end APIs to retrieve real-time data for bonds and funds, ensuring that the information displayed to the user was accurate and up-to-date.
        <li><b class="font-weight-bold">Problem-Solving</b></li>
            I independently developed a new interface to streamline the process of increasing non-contractual transfer limits. This required close technical collaboration to ensure the solution was secure and met regulatory requirements.
